# Risk profile guidance

A conservative investor generally prioritizes capital preservation and accepts lower expected growth in exchange for lower volatility. A moderate investor balances growth and stability across equities, bonds, cash, and real assets. An aggressive investor has a longer horizon and higher tolerance for volatility and potential loss, so a larger equity allocation may be appropriate.

Risk profiles are planning assumptions, not personal financial advice. A suitability review should be repeated when goals, income, liquidity needs, or time horizon change.
